How it works

ReadsThe tech's voice note and photos, or the job ticket from your field software, plus your price book, labour rates, tax codes and invoices in QuickBooks.
DraftsAI writes the work order, the invoice in your office's style, a plain-language report for the customer, and reminders at 7, 14 and 30 days.
FlagsMissing prices, serial numbers, warranty parts and purchase orders near their limit. It never guesses a price or a reading.
A person approvesThe office settles each flag and approves. Repairs the tech spotted go to the service manager to price.
NeverSends anything to a customer, sets a price, or touches payment details.
You own itEverything runs in your accounts. If it stops, you invoice the way you do today. No contract.

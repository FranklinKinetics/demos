How it works

ReadsQuotes and site-visit notes from your estimating or field software, plus your supplier price lists. A photo of the quote binder works for the first run.
DraftsAI writes the text, the call note or the last note, depending on how long the quote has waited.
FlagsExpired quotes and supplier price changes since the quote went out, so nobody follows up on a price you can't hold.
A person decidesYour office calls or sends, then logs won, lost or still deciding. Lost reasons add up over time.
NeverChanges a price, edits your estimates, or contacts a customer on its own.
You own itEverything runs in your accounts. If it stops, you follow up the way you do today. No contract.
